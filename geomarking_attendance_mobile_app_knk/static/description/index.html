GeoMarking Mobile App

GeoMarking is an attendance tracking app which uses GPS technology to register and track employee attendance. Upon starting/opening the app from being within the radius, employees are prompted to check in and out.

GeoMarking supports biometric attendance systems - facial and fingerprint authentication - for additional security. Employees can use their biometric data to check in or out, ensuring that only they can record their attendance. The app would also provide step-by-step instructions on setting up biometric authentication.
